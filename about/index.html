Hello! I'm Praditya Farell Raffa Faadilah.

I'm studying Artificial Intelligence at Purdue University, and I build software end-to-end — from the model and the data pipeline to the interface people actually touch. I also work as a graphic designer for PERMIAS Purdue, making event graphics and social content.

That mix is what I want to keep doing: AI that stays honest about what the data supports, wrapped in interfaces that feel effortless.
